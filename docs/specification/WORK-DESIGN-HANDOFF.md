## Visual acceptance criteria
The site must feel like:
**上質なアウトドア専門誌 × セレクトショップ × 休日の体験サービス**

It must NOT feel like:
- Rakuten affiliate portal
- price comparison site
- camping blog template
- children’s learning site
- generic SaaS questionnaire

## Visual hierarchy
1. Experience
2. Personal completion
3. Reasoned guidance
4. Product
5. Price

## Highest priority screens
1. Home HERO
2. Q6「何をしたい？」
3. Completion「キャンプ、できた。」
4. Product recommendation
5. Schedule

If any of the top three feel generic, stop and redesign before expanding the rest.
