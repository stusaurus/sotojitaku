# SOTOJITAKU Visual Asset Brief

## Core rule
All simulator scenes must look like the SAME PLACE photographed from the SAME CAMERA POSITION.
Do not make independent unrelated hero images.

## Art direction
- Japanese-friendly temperate forest campsite
- premium editorial outdoor photography
- natural light
- subdued saturation
- warm but not orange
- believable gear scale
- no visible brand logos
- no text in the image
- no camera-facing models
- no stock-photo smile poses
- no exaggerated luxury glamping
- no mountains dominating the scene unless subtle
- space left for UI copy
- mobile-safe crop and desktop-safe crop

## Scene 00 — Empty
Early morning.
A quiet, beautiful grassy campsite bordered by trees.
No tent, no chairs, no people.
Soft light, slightly cool air, calm anticipation.
This is "before the weekend begins."

## Scene 01 — People presence
Same camera, same place.
Subtle human presence only: small bags / silhouettes entering scene.
Do not fully populate camp yet.

## Scene 02 — Shelter
Same camera.
One appropriate beginner tent appears.
Keep composition calm and realistic.
Do not make the tent look like a product advertisement.

## Scene 03 — Relax
Same camera.
Add chairs and a table naturally.
Still daylight.
A believable beginner campsite, not an equipment showroom.

## Scene 04 — Food
Same camera.
Late afternoon.
Add cooking / dining cues depending on user choices.
Food should feel appetizing but not commercial food photography.

## Scene 05 — Evening
Same camera.
Dusk.
Lantern light begins.
No bonfire unless selected.

## Scene 06 — Complete
Same camera.
Blue hour to early evening.
The campsite feels finished and lived-in.
Warm lantern glow.
If bonfire selected, add a modest safe bonfire area.
If children selected, show only subtle age-appropriate play cues.
Leave visual breathing room for "キャンプ、できた。"

## Q6 tiles
Each tile must show the EXPERIENCE, not the tool:
- BBQ: shared food outdoors
- Bonfire: people quietly watching fire
- Morning coffee: steam in soft morning light
- Stargazing: calm dark sky / silhouettes
- Child play: simple outdoor play
- Cooking: hands preparing simple camp meal
- Relax: chair + book / resting
- Sleep comfort: cozy but realistic sleeping setup

## Do not
- use clip art
- use flat vector camping icons as primary scenes
- paste isolated products into a background
- show dangerous fire use
- use tent-interior fire
- overfill the campsite with gear
